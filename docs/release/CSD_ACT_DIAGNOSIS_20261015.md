## Finding

There is a real implementation defect in **our ACT preparation**, not evidence
that MRtrix CSD itself is broken. Legacy preparation incorrectly put bilateral
VentralDC labels 28/60 in subcortical GM and removed their WM probability.
It also left brainstem and cerebellar WM tissue identities to the three-class
DDSeg probabilities instead of explicit anatomical WM priors.

[MRtrix FreeSurfer2ACT](https://github.com/MRtrix3/mrtrix3/blob/3.0.8/share/mrtrix3/_5ttgen/FreeSurfer2ACT_sgm_amyg_hipp.txt)
maps VentralDC, brainstem and cerebellar WM to WM. The optional hippocampus/amygdala
sGM convention is retained. Our implementation remains a DDSeg partial-volume
hybrid, not an exact implementation of `5ttgen freesurfer`.
[ACT structural termination](https://github.com/MRtrix3/mrtrix3/blob/3.0.8/src/dwi/tractography/ACT/method.h)
distinguishes WM continuation from GM entry/exit. A numerical `5ttcheck` pass
alone cannot detect a wrong anatomical tissue identity.

In the saved 5TT, all 4242 VentralDC voxels had zero WM probability; 4214 were
sGM-dominant. Within brainstem label 16, 1302/10503 voxels were cGM-dominant.
These are predicted labels, not independently delineated ground truth.

## Controlled Evidence

Evidence is generated by `scripts/diagnose_csd_act.py` under
`ignore/validation/csd_diagnosis_20261015/`. Original FODs, parcellation and source
files captured in the reports retain their SHA256 values. No tracking threshold,
gradient orientation, WMA cluster assignment or sampling acceptance threshold is
relaxed to produce a green result.

The experiment reuses the exact saved normalized FOD that generated the failed
million-streamline input. Each arm attempts 10,000 seeds in the same eroded
brainstem ROI, with seed 20261015, iFOD2, 10-250 mm, cutoff 0.05 and one thread.
Equal RNG settings do not imply identical individual seed coordinates after
different tracking branches consume the random stream. This is a controlled
single-seed screening experiment, not a multi-seed statistical study.

| ACT condition | Accepted tracks | Brainstem + motor region, L/R | Median length |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Original v2 | 7661 | 0 / 0 | 62.863 mm |
| Only VentralDC corrected | 7636 | 72 / 127 | 81.066 mm |
| Full v3 WM-prior correction | 7637 | 71 / 115 | 110.177 mm |
| Without ACT, brain-mask termination | 9341 | 347 / 323 | 111.371 mm |

Counts refer to native point-label crossing proxies, **not** anatomically
certified CST or WMA output. This targeted seeding is not a whole-brain density
estimate. With only VentralDC corrected, endpoint occurrences in labels 28/60
fall from 1367/1471 to 22/36. The saved FOD can produce descending motor-region
connections once this erroneous tissue barrier is removed.

Tracking wall times are 79.027/91.092/226.092/101.423 seconds, respectively,
excluding preparation, hashing and plotting. The full correction permits longer
paths and takes longer in this targeted single-threaded experiment. These are
not default whole-brain runtime benchmarks. Removing ACT is a diagnostic control,
not the production fix; its larger candidate count does not prove better specificity.

The separate official `dwigradcheck` screen evaluates 48 hypotheses with 2000
tracks each. The two best hypotheses retain the original axes without flips
(mean lengths 60.65/60.50 mm); the best altered orientation gives 35.26 mm.
DW directions agree with the exported selected table to antipodal error
1.25e-10. The export zeros b0 b-values originally recorded as 5; no change is
applied to the input gradients. This heuristic supports, but cannot prove,
gradient correctness.

## Fix And Acceptance Boundary

- ACT preparation is now version 3. VentralDC is removed from sGM, and labels
  7/16/28/46/60 receive WM = 1 - P(CSF) inside DDSeg foreground. CSF partial
  volume and native world-space alignment are preserved; no holes are filled.
- Overlapping WM/sGM/CSF prior sets are rejected. Manifest records the mapping
  and per-label voxel counts. Corrected real 5TT passes MRtrix `5ttcheck`.
- Resume invalidates CSD outputs depending on legacy ACT versions. The
  independent scientific audit adds an ACT-preparation-current gate, including
  when a separate DTI tractography task is also present.
- Read-only re-audit of the previous joint graph now gives **164 passes and
  one failure** at this gate. The earlier 161-pass statement predates this
  check and is not current acceptance. Its original evidence is retained.

The old WMA CST 3/4 result remains failed. This experiment does not show that
the complete corrected CSD-to-WMA workflow has passed. Regenerate whole-brain
tracking, SIFT2 weights, affected bundles and connectomes, then repeat the
unchanged gates and expert anatomical review. Do not transfer the independent
UKF-to-WMA success to CSD data. UKF comparison does not depend on this ACT image.

Software regression: **1025 tests, 1024 passed and one explicit skip**. These
tests and targeted experiments do not replace full-route scientific acceptance.
